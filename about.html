History & Foundation

Our Story

From humble beginnings to a trusted financial institution

WB CREDIT UNION was founded on a simple yet powerful belief: that everyone deserves access to fair, transparent, and personalized financial services. What started as a small community-driven initiative has grown into a full-service credit union serving thousands of members across the globe.

Unlike traditional banks that answer to shareholders, WB CREDIT UNION is owned by its members. Every decision we make, every product we design, and every rate we offer is driven by one goal — your financial well-being.

Headquartered in the heart of Zurich, Switzerland, at 109 Feldgüetliweg Meilen, Bezirk Meilen, we combine the reliability of Swiss financial tradition with the innovation of modern digital banking. Our routing number 251480576 connects you to a network built on trust, security, and uncompromising service.

Today, WB CREDIT UNION offers a comprehensive suite of banking solutions — from high-yield savings and competitive loans to cutting-edge cryptocurrency wallets and multi-currency accounts. We serve individuals, families, and businesses with the same level of dedication and care.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to empower every member with accessible, innovative, and secure financial solutions that foster lasting prosperity. We are committed to delivering exceptional service, competitive rates, and cutting-edge technology that simplifies your financial life. At WB CREDIT UNION, we put people before profits — because your success is our success. Our Vision

Our vision is to redefine the future of banking by creating a world where financial services are inclusive, transparent, and seamlessly integrated into everyday life.
