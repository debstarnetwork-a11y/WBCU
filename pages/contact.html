Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions below. Can't find what you're looking for? Don't hesitate to contact us.

Opening an account with WB CREDIT UNION is quick and easy. Simply click 'Open Account' on our website, complete the four-step registration process, and your account will be active within minutes. You'll receive a welcome email with your account details and next steps.

Our routing number is 251480576. You'll need this number for direct deposits, wire transfers, and linking external accounts.

You can reset your password from the login page by clicking 'Forgot Password.' For PIN resets, please contact our support team at support@wbcu.net or visit your nearest branch with valid identification.

WB CREDIT UNION currently supports Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT, USDC, BNB, XRP, SOL, and ADA. You can enable crypto wallets during account registration or add them later from your dashboard.

Domestic wire transfers typically process within 1 business day. International wire transfers may take 1–3 business days depending on the destination country and verification requirements. You can track your transfer status in real-time from your dashboard.
