Our Commitment

At WB CREDIT UNION, we believe that everyone deserves equal access to financial services, regardless of ability. We are dedicated to ensuring that our website, mobile application, and digital banking platform are accessible to the widest possible audience, including individuals with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive disabilities.

We are actively working to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines provide a framework for making web content more accessible and usable for all people.

1. Accessibility Features

Our digital banking platform includes the following accessibility features:

1.1 Visual Accessibility

  • High-contrast color schemes that meet WCAG AA contrast ratio requirements (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text)
  • Resizable text up to 200% without loss of content or functionality
  • Alternative text descriptions for all images, icons, and graphical elements
  • Consistent and logical heading hierarchy (H1 through H6) for screen reader navigation
  • Color is never used as the sole means of conveying information
  • Dark mode option for users who prefer reduced brightness

1.2 Auditory Accessibility

  • All audio content includes text transcripts
  • Visual notifications supplement all audio alerts
  • Transaction notifications are delivered via email and in-app text alerts
  • Video content (when available) includes closed captions

1.3 Motor Accessibility

  • Full keyboard navigation support across all pages and interactive elements
  • Skip-to-main-content link for keyboard users
  • Focus indicators are clearly visible on all interactive elements
  • Touch targets are a minimum of 44x44 pixels for mobile users
  • No time-limited interactions without the option to extend or disable the time limit
  • Form inputs are properly labeled and associated with their labels

1.4 Cognitive Accessibility

  • Clear, simple, and consistent navigation structure
  • Predictable page layouts and interaction patterns
  • Error messages are descriptive and provide guidance for correction
  • Complex financial information is presented in plain language
  • Consistent terminology throughout the platform
  • Breadcrumb navigation on all pages

1.5 Assistive Technology Compatibility

  • Compatible with major screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack)
  • ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) labels and roles on all dynamic content
  • Proper form field associations and error announcements
  • Live region announcements for real-time balance updates and notifications

2. Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment, not a one-time achievement. WB CREDIT UNION takes the following steps to ensure continuous improvement:

  • Regular accessibility audits conducted by internal and external experts
  • User testing with individuals who use assistive technologies
  • Staff training on accessibility best practices and inclusive design
  • Integration of accessibility testing into our development workflow
  • Monitoring of user feedback and accessibility-related support requests
  • Periodic review and update of this Accessibility Statement

3. Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas of our platform may have limitations:

  • Third-party integrations (e.g., embedded maps, external payment processors) may not be fully accessible
  • PDF documents and downloadable statements may not be fully optimized for screen readers
  • Some legacy content may not yet meet current accessibility standards
  • Cryptocurrency wallet QR codes may require alternative text descriptions for screen reader users

We are actively working to address these limitations and welcome your feedback to help us prioritize improvements.

If you are not satisfied with our response to an accessibility concern, you may file a formal complaint with the relevant accessibility authority in your jurisdiction. We are committed to working with you to find a satisfactory resolution.
